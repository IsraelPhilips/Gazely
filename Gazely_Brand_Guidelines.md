# Gazely Brand Guidelines

**Version 1.0 --- September 2026**

> **Primary descriptor:** Intelligence for live presentation.\
> **Expressive line:** Gazely pays attention. So you can stay present.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Brand Overview

**Gazely** is an intelligent layer for live presentation software. It
pays attention to what is happening in a live environment, understands
relevant context, and helps bring the right content into view at the
right moment.

Gazely is not positioned as a replacement for presentation software. It
augments the tools presentation teams already use with context-aware
intelligence.

### Core brand idea

**Attentive intelligence.**

Gazely should feel intelligent without being loud about being "AI." The
brand is calm, observant, precise, dependable and present.

### Brand philosophy

**Attention → Understanding → Presentation**

1.  **Attention** --- Gazely pays attention to what is happening live.
2.  **Understanding** --- Gazely interprets the moment and identifies
    what is relevant.
3.  **Presentation** --- Gazely helps bring the right content into view.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. The Name

The name **Gazely** is inspired by the idea of **gaze**: attention,
awareness, focus and what comes into view.

The name should not be explained as literal visual surveillance.
Gazely's broader idea is *paying attention* to a live moment,
understanding it and helping the operator respond.

### Short brand explanation

> Gazely comes from the idea of "gazing" --- paying attention to what is
> happening and knowing what should be brought into view.

### Product naming

-   **Master brand:** Gazely
-   **Product descriptor, when needed:** Gazely Presenter
-   **Integration naming:** Gazely for EasyWorship, Gazely for
    ProPresenter, Gazely for FreeShow

Do not create separate sub-brands for integrations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Logo Philosophy

The Gazely symbol is composed of **two interacting flowing forms**
separated by deliberate negative space.

### Primary meaning

The **upper form** represents what Gazely receives and pays attention
to: the live moment, speech, context and incoming information.

The **lower form** represents what Gazely brings forward: the Scripture,
song, media, cue or other presentation content that should come into
view.

The **negative space between the forms** represents the intelligence
layer: the understanding that connects what is happening to what should
be presented.

In its simplest conceptual form:

**Input → Intelligence → Output**

or, in brand language:

**Attention → Understanding → Presentation**

### The "pseudo-G"

Taken together, the two forms also create a loose, abstract **G-like
gesture**, providing a secondary visual connection to **Gazely**.

This should remain a subtle discovery rather than an explicit
letterform. The symbol must **not** be redrawn merely to make the G more
obvious. Its abstraction is part of its character.

### What the symbol is not

The logo should not officially be described as: - an eye or eyeball; -
an eyebrow; - two leaves; - wings; - a microphone; - a Bible; - a
projector; - an AI sparkle.

Viewers may naturally perceive other shapes in the abstraction. Those
readings do not need to be suppressed, but they are **not the governing
brand rationale**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Logo System

The approved logo system consists of:

### Primary full-colour logo

-   Gazely symbol on the left.
-   Gazely wordmark on the right.
-   Upper symbol form: Gazely Black.
-   Lower symbol form: Signal Lime.
-   Wordmark: Gazely Black.
-   Intended for white or Porcelain backgrounds.

### Reversed full-colour logo

-   Upper form: white.
-   Lower form: Signal Lime.
-   Wordmark: white.
-   Intended for Gazely Black or sufficiently dark backgrounds.

### Full-black logo

-   Both symbol forms and the wordmark are solid black/dark.
-   Use when colour reproduction is unavailable or inappropriate.

### Full-white logo

-   Both symbol forms and the wordmark are white.
-   Use on sufficiently dark backgrounds when Signal Lime is unavailable
    or a monochrome treatment is required.

### Symbol-only

Use for: - application icons; - system tray; - favicons; - social
avatars; - compact UI placements; - contexts where the Gazely brand is
already established.

### Wordmark-only

Use sparingly where the symbol is impractical or where a purely
typographic treatment is required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Logo Construction and Consistency

The selected Gazely logo is a **custom brand mark**.

The wordmark seen in the approved visual identity should be treated as a
**custom wordmark**, not as ordinary typed text. The original generated
lettering resembles a bold geometric/grotesk sans, but it is not
reliably attributable to one commercial font.

Therefore:

-   Do not recreate the logo by typing "Gazely" in Geist, Inter, Söhne
    or another approximate font.
-   Maintain one approved vector master for the symbol and custom
    wordmark.
-   All production logo exports should derive from that master.
-   Supporting typography and the logo wordmark are separate systems.

### Clear space

Maintain generous clear space around the logo. As a baseline, use at
least **25% of the symbol width** on every side of the complete lockup.

More space is preferable in premium brand applications.

### Minimum size

Recommended starting thresholds: - Symbol-only: **16 px** minimum
digital size. - Horizontal lockup: **96 px** minimum digital width.

At very small sizes, use the symbol-only version and verify optical
clarity.

### Never

Do not: - stretch or compress the logo; - rotate it; - change the
relative proportions of the two forms; - close or substantially alter
the negative space; - add outlines, shadows, glows or gradients; -
recolour individual forms arbitrarily; - place the logo over visually
noisy imagery without sufficient contrast; - add an eye pupil or make
the symbol anatomically eye-like; - add an explicit G stroke; - combine
the Gazely mark physically with a partner logo.

## 6. Colour System

### Gazely Black

**HEX:** `#111310`\
**RGB:** `17, 19, 16`

Primary dark surface and principal brand neutral. It is intentionally
warmer than absolute black.

### Porcelain

**HEX:** `#F3F1EA`\
**RGB:** `243, 241, 234`

Primary warm light surface. Prefer it over sterile white where the
application allows.

### Signal Lime

**HEX:** `#B8F34A`\
**RGB:** `184, 243, 74`

Gazely's principal accent.

Signal Lime is **semantic**, not merely decorative. It represents
moments when Gazely has noticed, recognized, understood or prepared
something.

Typical uses: - recognized/ready states; - active intelligence; -
positive confirmation; - selective calls to action; - small emphasis
elements.

Avoid flooding large surfaces with Signal Lime. Its strength comes from
restraint.

### Stone

**HEX:** `#6B6F68`\
**RGB:** `107, 111, 104`

Supporting neutral for secondary text, dividers and subdued information.

### White

**HEX:** `#FFFFFF`

Use where clean white is required, particularly for reversed logo
applications.

### General colour balance

Gazely should predominantly feel **black / warm neutral / white**, with
Signal Lime appearing deliberately.

A useful principle is:

> Neutral by default. Signal when Gazely has noticed something.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Typography

### Logo typography

The **Gazely wordmark is custom artwork**. It is not the brand's body
typeface and should not be reconstructed from a font.

### Supporting typography

Recommended production families:

**Primary recommendation: Geist Sans**\
**Fallback / UI alternative: Inter**

Both provide a contemporary, clean, highly legible technology aesthetic
without competing with the custom wordmark.

Suggested hierarchy: - Display / hero: 600--700 - Section headings:
600--700 - Body: 400--500 - UI labels: 500--600 - Small metadata: 500
with restrained positive tracking where appropriate

Avoid: - exaggerated futuristic fonts; - sci-fi display typography; -
excessively rounded "friendly SaaS" typography; - extreme letter
spacing; - using many font families together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Graphic Language

Gazely's graphic system should communicate **focus, precision and quiet
intelligence**.

### Core principles

-   generous negative space;
-   high contrast;
-   strong alignment;
-   restrained grids;
-   thin structural rules;
-   cropped/enlarged forms derived from the Gazely symbol;
-   deliberate rather than decorative use of Signal Lime;
-   simple surfaces with a premium technology feel.

The two master symbol forms may inspire framing devices, masks and large
abstract crops, but the actual logo must never be distorted to create
them.

### Avoid

Do not build the visual system around: - literal eyes; - microphones; -
Bibles or crosses as default brand graphics; - projector icons; - brains
or robots; - circuit-board heads; - AI sparkles; - lightning bolts; -
generic purple/blue AI gradients; - excessive glassmorphism or neon
cyberpunk styling.

Gazely should look like **serious presentation technology**, not generic
"AI software."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Product UI Foundation

The product interface should feel like an extension of the brand rather
than a separate design language.

### Surfaces

Dark/near-black should be the natural primary application environment,
particularly for use in production booths and low-light venues.

Light mode can use Porcelain and white.

### Signal Lime in the product

Use Signal Lime for meaningful states such as: - recognized; - ready; -
active; - confirmed; - selected high-confidence action.

Do not communicate state through colour alone. Pair colour with text,
iconography or both.

### Suggested geometry

-   Base spacing unit: **4 px**
-   Common spacing: **8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 px**
-   Controls: approximately **10 px radius**
-   Cards: approximately **14 px radius**
-   Large surfaces/dialogues: approximately **18--22 px radius**

These are foundation values, not immutable rules. Optical balance takes
priority.

### Product behavior

The interface should reinforce the operating philosophy:

> Invisible when it is not needed. Instant when it is needed. Never
> fights the operator.

Human/operator actions have priority over automation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Motion Identity

### Principle

**Focus, then reveal.**

Gazely motion should feel attentive rather than energetic.

### Listening

Use a subtle, steady presence. Avoid aggressive microphone-style
pulsing.

### Recognition

When Gazely understands something, the visual system may subtly tighten,
align or come into focus.

### Ready / confirmation

Signal Lime can appear with a restrained transition to indicate that
something meaningful has been recognized or prepared.

### Content reveal

Prefer: - subtle fades; - approximately 6--10 px translation; -
controlled focus transitions.

Indicative timing: - micro-interactions: **160--240 ms** - contextual
reveals: **220--320 ms** - larger transitions: **280--420 ms**

Respect reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

Avoid: - bouncing; - spinning; - perpetual glowing; - chatbot-like
pulsing; - AI sparkle animations; - attention-seeking loops.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. Photography and Art Direction

Gazely imagery should feel **cinematic but real**.

### Preferred imagery

-   real live-presentation environments;
-   projection/control booths;
-   operators at work;
-   screens in context;
-   directional stage lighting;
-   subtle haze;
-   dark auditoriums;
-   controlled highlights;
-   close crops of presentation technology;
-   negative space suitable for typography.

### Avoid

-   stereotypical worship stock photography;
-   generic raised-hands imagery as the main brand visual;
-   overused preacher/cross imagery;
-   fake holograms;
-   generic AI brains;
-   cyberpunk environments;
-   obviously staged "tech team smiling at camera" photography.

The imagery should communicate that Gazely is a **serious technology
product that happens to solve live presentation problems**, initially
with a strong church use case.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 12. Brand Messaging

### Primary descriptor

> **Intelligence for live presentation.**

This is the primary line directly associated with the Gazely identity.

### Expressive brand line

> **Gazely pays attention. So you can stay present.**

This is a supporting marketing line. It may appear alongside the primary
descriptor when the layout has sufficient hierarchy and space.

### Product explanation

> Gazely pays attention to what is happening during a live presentation,
> understands the context, and helps bring the right content into view
> at the right moment.

### AI/product one-liner

> AI-powered intelligence that listens to live presentation context and
> prepares the right content for the screen.

### Church-focused explanation

> Gazely listens to what is happening during a service, understands the
> context, and helps the presentation team bring the right Scripture or
> content into view.

### Brand voice

Gazely should sound: - calm; - clear; - confident; - intelligent; -
practical; - technically credible; - unobtrusive.

Gazely should **not** sound: - breathlessly futuristic; - magical; -
overly corporate; - gimmicky; - robotic; - hype-driven.

Prefer describing what the product actually does over repeatedly calling
it "revolutionary," "next-generation" or "AI-powered."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 13. Tagline Hierarchy

When both primary lines appear together, use this hierarchy:

**Gazely**

**INTELLIGENCE FOR LIVE PRESENTATION**

*Gazely pays attention. So you can stay present.*

The primary descriptor should have stronger visual hierarchy than the
expressive line.

Do not replace the primary descriptor with internal strategy language
such as "From Attention to Understanding."

**Attention → Understanding → Presentation** is a brand philosophy, not
the public tagline.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 14. Integration Branding

Approved naming pattern:

-   **Gazely for EasyWorship**
-   **Gazely for ProPresenter**
-   **Gazely for FreeShow**

Gazely remains visually dominant.

Partner marks, when their brand guidelines permit use, should remain
visually independent. Separate the two brands with appropriate spacing
or structural devices rather than constructing a hybrid logo.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 15. Digital Applications

### App icon

Use the Gazely symbol without the wordmark.

Preferred treatment: - Gazely Black background; - white upper form; -
Signal Lime lower form.

The icon must be optically tested at: - 16 px; - 20/24 px; - 32 px; - 48
px; - 64 px; - 128 px; - 256 px; - 512 px and above.

Small system-tray treatments may require a simplified monochrome symbol
where platform conventions demand it.

### Social avatar

Use the symbol-only treatment. Avoid shrinking the full horizontal
wordmark into a square avatar.

### Favicon

Use a simplified symbol-only master and test at 16 and 32 px.

### Open Graph / social sharing

Recommended canvas: **1200 × 630 px**.

Prefer: - large Gazely identity; - primary descriptor; - strong negative
space; - minimal supporting information.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 16. Accessibility and Contrast

Brand aesthetics must not override usability.

-   Maintain WCAG-appropriate contrast for functional text and controls.
-   Never use Signal Lime as small body text on a light background
    without verifying contrast.
-   Do not rely on colour alone to communicate recognition, errors,
    warnings or live states.
-   Respect reduced-motion preferences.
-   Preserve legibility in low-light production environments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 17. Brand Essence

If the entire Gazely identity has to be reduced to one idea:

> **Gazely pays attention to the live moment, understands what matters,
> and helps bring the right thing into view.**

The identity should always feel like the product itself:

**quietly attentive, context-aware, precise and ready when needed.**
