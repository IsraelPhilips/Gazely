GAZELY
Descriptor: Intelligence for live presentation.

Brand idea:
Gazely pays attention to what is happening live, understands the context, and helps bring the right content into view.

Brand philosophy:
Attention -> Understanding -> Presentation.

Short explanation of the name:
Gazely is coined around “gaze”: attention, awareness and what comes into view. It represents software that pays attention to a live moment and helps surface the right content at the right time.

Primary one-liner:
AI-powered intelligence that listens to live presentation context and prepares the right content for the screen.

Expressive lines:
Gazely pays attention. So you can stay present.
It listens. It understands. It brings the right thing into view.

Naming:
Master brand: Gazely
Product descriptor when needed: Gazely Presenter
Integrations: Gazely for EasyWorship / Gazely for ProPresenter / Gazely for FreeShow
